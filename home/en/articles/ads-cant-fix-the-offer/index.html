Advertising can’t fix an offer that isn’t ready

Rima Yaqub, November 2026, 4 min read

“I tried ads, and they didn’t work.” I hear it often, and it’s usually true. But the problem was rarely the ad.

Advertising is an amplifier. It takes what already exists and puts it in front of more people. If the offer is clear and the price is built on value, it amplifies the result. If not, it amplifies the problem and burns money faster.

Signs the offer isn’t ready yet

The service is described as a number of sessions. “Five sessions for this price” tells the client nothing about what will change in their life or business.

Every inquiry turns into a price question. When value isn’t clear, price becomes the only thing left to compare.

There is no path after the click. The ad brings a message, then nothing: no follow-up, no diagnosis, no clear next step.

The right order

In the RIMA Method, attracting clients is stage six, not stage one. Before it come diagnosis, positioning, packages, pricing and message. Once those are in place, advertising becomes a measurable investment rather than a gamble.

Before asking how much to spend on ads, ask: is what I’m about to promote ready to be bought?
