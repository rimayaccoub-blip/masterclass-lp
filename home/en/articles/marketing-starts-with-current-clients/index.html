Marketing starts with the client you already have

Rima Yaqub, November 2026, 4 min read

Most marketing budgets go to people who don’t know you yet. Very little goes to the person who already bought from you and was happy with the result.

What the numbers say

Research by Frederick Reichheld at Bain & Company, summarised in his book The Loyalty Effect, showed that increasing client retention by just 5% can raise profits by 25% to 95%, depending on the industry.

The reason is that an existing client doesn’t need an ad to find you or persuasion to trust you. They tend to buy more over time, and they bring others.

What this means for a service business

A path after the service. What happens after the last session? If the answer is “nothing”, income is leaking.

A continuation offer. A follow-up package or monthly plan for clients who got a result and want to keep it.

Asking for referrals. A satisfied client is the strongest marketing channel, but rarely refers on their own. They need a clear invitation.

Advertising still matters

Attracting new clients is essential for any growing business. But when a system keeps clients after the purchase, every new client becomes more valuable, and every unit spent on ads returns more.

Before your next campaign, ask: what do I offer the people who have already bought from me?

Sources: Frederick F. Reichheld, The Loyalty Effect, Harvard Business School Press, 1996. Bain & Company.
