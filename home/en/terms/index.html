Terms & cancellation policy

Last updated: November 2026

Focus Session: cancellation & rescheduling

Sessions can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

If you cancel within 14 days of purchase and at least 48 hours before the scheduled session, you will receive a full refund within 14 days of your cancellation request.

Cancellations made more than 14 days after purchase or less than 48 hours before the session, and missed sessions without notice, are not eligible for a refund.

To cancel or reschedule, contact us by email or WhatsApp.

Refund policy for advisory programs

If you have participated in the sessions and can demonstrate that you have fully implemented the strategies and steps provided, yet have seen no tangible results or progress within 30 days of your purchase, we will issue a full refund of your payment. This guarantee does not apply to the Focus Session, which is covered by the cancellation policy above.

Nature of the work

The advisory relationship is business consulting. It is in no way to be construed as psychological counseling or psychotherapy. If you feel the need for professional counseling or therapy, it is your responsibility to seek a licensed professional.

Results are not guaranteed. You enter the work with the understanding that results depend on implementation and that you are responsible for creating your own results.

Confidentiality

In the course of our work, you may share future plans, financial, professional and personal information, goals, and other proprietary information about your business.
