The premise
Most service businesses aren’t short of clients. They’re short of a model that turns expertise into an offer, and an offer into profit.

The gap

The difference between a business that works and one that profits

The expertise is there, clients are satisfied and the effort is real. Yet every month starts from zero. The problem is rarely skill. It is how the business itself is built.

About

I build businesses from the numbers up

I’m Rima Yaqub, a business strategist. I work with service business owners who have real expertise, but whose business doesn’t yet reflect it in income and profit.

I came to business strategy from finance. Managing accounts, cash flow and financial reporting taught me what many marketers miss: revenue is not profit, and growth without a system is fragile. I later led an economic empowerment program and a team that guided hundreds of women in building their own income.

Today I work with clinics, wellness centers and experts across service industries, and have helped them grow revenue by 150% to 300% and beyond. I lead this work through LeverageGrow360, under my firm, Rima Yaqub Consulting LLC.

That’s why I don’t start with advertising. I start with the numbers. Every business I work with gets a clear financial file that shows where the money goes, which service truly profits, and what the next decision should be.

Questions

Frequently asked

How is this different from a marketing agency?

An agency is responsible for the advertising. I work on the business that receives it: the offer, the price, the packages and the sales path. Advertising is part of the work, not the whole of it.

Are results guaranteed?

No, and no one can honestly guarantee them, because part of the outcome depends on execution. What you will leave with: a clear model, value-based pricing, and a system you know how to run.

Is the work in person or remote?

All work is remote, so it suits business owners across the Arab world and internationally, in Arabic or English.

Where do I start?

With a Focus Session. We diagnose where you are, and you’ll know whether you need a full partnership, a focused program, or clear steps to continue on your own.
